The Crypto Farm is an ATM where you may use your money to invest in cryptocoin.
Its main purpose is to allow the farmer to amass wealth greater than the 999,999,999 game limit.
Install this ATM in your map, use your surplus funds to buy some cryptocoins (paying a small fee), and watch your fortune grow (no guarantees - remember: buy low, sell high).
It won't change the 999,999,999 bank account limit, in the game, but you may endup with much more than that if you keep the surplus money in cryptocoins.
